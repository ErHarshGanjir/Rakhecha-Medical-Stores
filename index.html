Expiry Breakage Claim Process

Expiry breakage claim is a complimentary service for our valuable customers. This service provides value for eligible expired or damaged products, subject to the applicable terms and conditions.

01

Handover Products

Hand over expired or damaged products to the RMS executive with a tag bearing your name or organization.

02

Verification & Eligibility

The product will be checked and eligibility verified. Approval is subject to the applicable terms and conditions.

03

Generate Credit Note

A credit note will be generated for the approved eligible claim.

04

Adjust on Future Purchases

The approved credit note will be adjusted against future purchases.

Terms and Conditions for Expiry Breakage Claim

  • ✓ Products should be purchased from Rakhecha Medical Stores.
  • ✓ Expired products must be handed over to the RMS executive within 5 months of expiry.
  • ✓ For damaged or broken medicines, batch numbers must be clearly visible.
